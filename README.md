# 🎵 MG Music

A personal music player inspired by **Ado**, created with passion,
respect, and admiration for music.

Un reproductor de música personal inspirado en **Ado**, creado con pasión,
respeto y admiración por la música.

---

## 🌌 About the project (EN)

MG Music is a personal music player project.
It includes unique visual and experience-based behaviors when songs by
Ado are detected, while still functioning as a normal music player for
all other artists.

The application does not require monetization to function.
Any optional support or donation feature is completely voluntary.

This is **NOT an official application** and has no affiliation with Ado
or her management team.

---

MG Music es un proyecto de reproductor de música personal.
